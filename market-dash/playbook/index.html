The rules

RuleWhat it does
TrapYesterday's candle is with the trade and QQQ is 1.00+ past yesterday's low (calls) or high (puts). Buy every EMA8 turn back the trade's way.
Gap-and-goGap of 2.50+, nearest GEX strike 1.00+ away, last week's candle with the trade. Max 2 a day, 30 min apart.
Fade the weekEMA8 turn against last week's candle, after 7:30, only if QQQ was inside yesterday's value area at 7:00, no trap zone seen, no gap-and-go armed, and no other trade taken.
Stop0.50 under what you paid.
Re-entryAfter a stop, buy the next same-side EMA8 turn within 45 min, only if QQQ has not broken the swing it came off by more than 1.00.
Fake-out lineOnce QQQ breaks that swing by 1.00, no more buys on that side for the rest of the day.
FlipAfter the fake-out line breaks, buy the other side on the next EMA8 turn. Max 1 a day.
ExitsAt +0.30 the stop moves to −0.15. At +0.50 the trail arms: sell on the first EMA8 tick against. Time exit at 90 min or 8:56.
Limits1 contract. Max 2 open, trap wins conflicts, 10 min between buys. 2 stops in a day = done.

Every setting moved up to ±20% at once: median +$2,831, never negative. 10,000 shuffles: about 3–5% chance of a losing 2 weeks, under 1% of losing $150+.
ContractNearest the money, mid 0.80–1.25, IV ≤ 35% is the sweet spot. Cheaper (0.64–1.00) −$476; pricier (0.96–1.50) −$1,533.
More contractsEach contract earns the same, so drawdowns grow with size. Adding a contract at +0.30 only wins if you click the same minute; 1–2 min late, 2 from the start is as good or better. Budget fits 1 contract.
VIXMakes money at every VIX level seen (14–21; nothing known above 25). Red day + VIX ≥ 16.5 → QQQ up by 9:00 on 75% of mornings (calm red days 36%). Candidate v9.3 “VIX bounce calls” (call turns after 7:30, 1 a day) +$3,585 — tracked in the paper log only.
How QQQ movesOption ≈ 0.45 per 1.00 of QQQ. Winners: QQQ +1.97 at the exit; losers −0.84; home runs +5.36. Typical morning range 6.32. Winners are usually +0.45 by minute 5 and +1.00 by ~11; losers −0.50 by ~2 min and −1.00 by ~8. Observation only: as exit rules these made things worse.
Puts (on hold)+$916 vs calls +$2,401. Puts snap back (give back 0.91 of their peak); faster exits didn’t help; no reliable put signal after green days yet.
Overnight next-day contractsNo proven edge yet: the 12:55 price decides the result, and the 5 real nights only paid on earnings nights. The logger now saves next-day contracts, pre-market (4:30–6:30 AM PT) and the after-close session, so this gets retested on real data.
